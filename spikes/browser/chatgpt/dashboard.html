What the evidence means

Saved locally: the selected text bytes and the client's observation are retained in your encrypted vault. A normal Send observation does not control sending.


Anchor corroborated: local checks compared independent sources. This is not yet a portable consensus proof or an independently verified timestamp.


Portable proof retained: an archival proof is included for independent verification with a separately trusted checkpoint.


These records do not prove authorship, ownership, provider receipt, complete conversation history or model reasoning. Historical cancellations and uncertain sends retain their original meaning. They cannot erase provider copies or published anchors.
